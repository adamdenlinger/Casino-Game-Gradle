1. At the start of the game, you press the slow or fast button to decide how many times you would like to play the game
2. if you chose fast it would run 10,000 times and give you the results like how much money you gained/losed and how many games you won

(GUI) Menu Screen - 2 buttons
 [SLOW]  &  [FAST]

1. Disposes MenuFrame
2. Creates new GameSession session
    -Player

    -Deck
3. Creates SlowFrame with GameSession
